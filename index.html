When you ask queries via the AI Astrologer text chat or live voice call feature:

  • Encrypted Transmission: Astrological context parameters (planetary placements, Lagna, Dasa periods) and your consultation query are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS to secure cloud-based Artificial Intelligence (AI) and automated Vedic natural language processing systems solely to generate the astrological guidance.
  • Quality & Compliance Logging: Consultation queries and AI responses are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS and may be reviewed confidentially by our internal administrative team solely to verify prediction accuracy, monitor compliance with strict ethical/legal guardrails (including prohibitions against fetal sex determination under the PCPNDT Act, suicide/self-harm, and defamation), and enhance guidance quality.
  • No Advertising Profiling: We strictly do not sell, rent, or use consultation transcripts for commercial ad profiling or third-party marketing.

D. Audio & Microphone Access (Live Voice Call)

The application requests microphone permission (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO) solely when you actively initiate and interact with the Live AI Astrologer voice call feature to speak your question. Your spoken audio is streamed in real time via encrypted HTTPS directly for speech recognition and astrological answer generation. Audio is processed transiently and is never recorded in the background or permanently stored on our servers.
