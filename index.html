1. Introduction

Welcome to Vaibhavalakshmi Tamil Astro ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information that you provide when using our mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, stores, and protects your information, and outlines your rights in relation to your personal data in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

2. Information We Collect and Process

A. Astrological Input Data (Provided by You)

To compute authentic Vedic astrological charts, Panchangam, Dasa-Bhukti-Antharam timelines, and marriage matching compatibility, you may optionally input:

  • Name and Family Details (Person Name, Father's Name)
  • Birth Particulars (Date of Birth, Time of Birth)
  • Birth Place (City, State, Country, Latitude & Longitude)
  • Gender (for Marriage Matching evaluation)

Local Storage: All entered birth records are stored strictly locally on your device via Android local storage (SharedPreferences). We do NOT upload, sync, or sell your birth records to any external cloud database.

B. Astrologer Office Branding Settings

If you enter your Astrologer Center details (Center Name, Contact Phone Number, Top Invocation), these details remain on your device only to personalize your exported Single-Page Horoscope and 10-Page Brihat Horoscope PDF reports.

C. AI Astrologer Consultations

When you ask queries via the AI Astrologer feature, astrological context parameters (planetary placements, Lagna, Dasa period) and your specific question are securely transmitted via encrypted HTTPS to Google Generative AI services solely to generate the astrological interpretation.

D. App Analytics & Technical Data (Google Firebase Analytics)

To monitor app performance, screen stability, and feature usage patterns, we use Google Firebase Analytics:

  • Aggregated Interaction Data: We track anonymous aggregated metrics such as app open events, screen views (e.g. Panchangam, Horoscope charts, or Marriage Matching), and general usage durations.
  • Zero Personal Identification: No names, phone numbers, birth records, or consultation texts are ever sent to analytics events. All records are pseudonymous and aggregated.
  • Encrypted Transmission: All data is securely transmitted via HTTPS directly to Google's protected analytics infrastructure.

3. What We DO NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect financial account numbers, credit/debit card numbers, or banking credentials.
  • We do NOT access your device contacts, microphone, camera, SMS, or media gallery.
  • We do NOT track your GPS location in the background.

4. Advertising & Data Sharing

Version 1.0.0 is completely ad-free. There are no advertising trackers or marketing analytics active in this release. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers or third parties.

5. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data. All external network communications use secure HTTPS / SSL encryption, and local customer records remain isolated within Android's sandboxed storage.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

You have full control over your stored records:

  • You can delete individual saved customer charts at any time from the app's Customer Profiles manager.
  • Uninstalling the app or choosing Clear Storage / Clear Data in Android Settings permanently removes all saved horoscopes from your phone.
